# Employee Turnover Analysis using R

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition using R Programming to understand patterns and factors associated with employees leaving an organization.

## Dataset
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.

Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset

## Objectives
- Understand employee attrition patterns.
- Analyze turnover across different departments.
- Examine factors such as overtime, monthly income, job satisfaction, and years at the company.
- Identify important findings and suggest employee retention strategies.

## Tools & Technologies
- R Programming
- Tidyverse
- Janitor
- skimr
- ggplot2
- RStudio 

## Project Workflow
1. Data Importing
2. Data Cleaning
3. Exploratory Data Analysis
4. Data Visualization

## Visualizations
The project includes visualizations exploring employee turnover across different factors.

## Key findings
- Department-wise analysis shows differences in employee turnover across departments. The bar graph shows highest turnover rate across Sales department compared to HR and Research department.
- Employees working overtime have higher attrition rate compared to those who do not.
- Employees who left and those who stayed may have different average monthly incomes.
- Job satisfaction levels show varying attrition rates.
- Employees who left may have different average years of experience at the company.

## Conclusion
The analysis provides insights into employee attrition patterns and highlights areas where organizations can focus on improving employee retention.
